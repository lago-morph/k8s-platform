# Session capture — the 2026-06-10 forensics and keystone session (resumed 2026-09-13)

**What this is.** A capture of the ideas exchanged in one long Claude Code
session, grouped by topic rather than by time. The session opened on
2026-06-10 in a web sandbox with a forensic look at why the project kept
failing. That led to an instruction-surface restructure (PRs #215–#217), then
the ADR-0010 cluster-facts keystone work and its retrospective (PRs #218–#219).
It was resumed on 2026-09-13: first in the web session, for a first capture
attempt, then on J's local WSL machine for a few small exchanges and this redo.
The capture holds what was said, including
suggestions that were narrowed, withdrawn, superseded or never answered, so the
whole context can be picked up again later. Both voices are compressed. Key
phrases are quoted exactly as they appear in the source.

**What this is not.** Not a design, not a prioritized list, not a set of
decisions. It is not the retrospective either
(`retrospective/2026-06-10-218.md` turns the session into lessons; this keeps
the conversation). Most of J's input was either a choice among options A
offered or a direct instruction to act (commit, merge, write, retrospect).
The one choice the conversation framed as a decision was the ratification of
ADR-0010. A framed it that way ("this changes a recorded decision, so it's
yours to call"), and J picked the ratify option (§11). Everything else J said
about the project, however firmly, was an opinion at that point in time.
Lines marked *stated position* are the ones put most firmly. They are not
settled.

Attribution: **J** is Jonathan, the repo owner. **A** is the agent. Lines
marked **A (interpretation)** are the agent's read of intent, mood or values.
They are context for the words, not words J said. `[event]` items (PR merges,
CI results, harness messages) did not come from J.

## 11. ADR-0010: the cluster-facts mechanism and its adversarial review

- **A's finding on reading the tree:** "the fix is better-positioned than the
  handoff suggested." A hub-side producer chain already existed (terraform →
  `cluster-network` EnvironmentConfig; Compositions already surface
  `certificateArn` in XR status). auto-008 had already put one fact on the
  spoke's ArgoCD cluster Secret as an annotation. "All nine placeholder-carrying
  spoke apps reduce to the same four facts: domain, cert ARN, external-dns role
  ARN, region."
- **A's draft, which A labelled "Decided mechanism (drafted as ADR-0010)"
  before J ratified:** facts ride the spoke cluster Secret as a labelled
  annotation contract. The nine per-app Applications become ApplicationSets
  that template those facts into helm values. A said this "dissolves the
  bootstrap-selfHeal fight structurally", keeps workloads cloud-agnostic (hello
  sees only `domain`), and stays static-only in this PR. `argocd/**` is not on
  the live-evidence gate's trigger paths, and the producer half would land later
  where the gate applies.
- **Conflict with the recorded decision:** ADR-0005's literal "ConfigMap read by
  add-ons" wording was, per A, infeasible for two of the four facts, because
  Service and ServiceAccount annotations cannot read ConfigMaps. A kept ADR-0005's
  principle but changed the carrier, and said "that contradiction is exactly why
  I'll ask you to ratify before implementing."
- **Adversarial review:** A dispatched two tree-grounded reviewers, one on
  ArgoCD/ApplicationSet mechanics and one on contract completeness and producer
  feasibility. The draft was checkpointed first (`76181cd`).
- **The mechanics review, as A relayed it:** "three of my claims don't survive
  the tree."
  - The kubeconform promise was "hollow": no ApplicationSet schema was ever
    fetched, and the CRD store was pinned to a different ArgoCD version than
    the deployed chart.
  - The cutover is "genuinely non-atomic": bootstrap prunes the old Applications
    and generated ones re-sync from scratch. The draft had "papered over" that
    outage window.
  - The hub-side alloy app "structurally cannot share an ApplicationSet with the
    spoke apps" (different project and destination). auto-008 had explicitly

## 13. Why the ApplicationSet schema is 24,000 lines

- **J:** "Why is kubeconform-schemas/argoproj.io/applicationset_v1alpha1.json
  24000 lines?"
- **A** measured. One Application spec (`application_v1alpha1.json`) is 5,840
  lines. The ApplicationSet schema has 25 `"syncPolicy"` occurrences and 48
  `"valuesObject"`, i.e. ~25 inlined copies of the Application spec. The copies
  come from:
  - `spec.template` (1);
  - a per-generator `template` override on each of 10 generator types (10);
  - `matrix` and `merge` nesting child generators one level deep (roughly 14).

  Root cause: "OpenAPI schemas inside CRDs cannot use `$ref`". The upstream CRD
  YAML is itself ~580 KB, and the fetch script adds only the `$schema` header,
  `additionalProperties: false` hardening and pretty-printing.
- **A's characterization:** "bulky but correct, and the bulk is load-bearing".
  Those copies let kubeconform reject unknown fields inside an ApplicationSet
  template, which is the gap (B1 in the mechanics review) the schema was added
  to close. The cost is one-time repo weight with no meaningful slowdown.
- **J** did not follow up. *Unaddressed.*
